Privacy Policy

Area Annotator for Google Maps · Last updated: November 26, 2026

Area Annotator for Google Maps ("the extension") is made by John Elro Karl Estoque.

What the extension stores

The extension stores the areas you draw on your own device, using the browser's local extension storage (chrome.storage.local). This includes their names, numbers, colors and coordinates.

What the extension does not do

  • It does not collect, send or sell any personal or usage data.
  • It has no servers, analytics, tracking or advertising.
  • It does not read your Google account, search history or anything else on the page beyond the map's position in the address bar. It uses that position to place your shapes correctly.

Exports

The data only leaves your browser when you click Copy or Download. It then goes to your clipboard or to a file on your computer, which you control.

Deleting your data

Delete areas in the extension's panel, or uninstall the extension. Uninstalling removes all stored data.
